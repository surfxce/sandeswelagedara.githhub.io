What I'm building

Mostly one thing, properly.

2026 — ongoing

Beyond15

A suite of apps designed to augment the healthcare experience, from patient to clinic. What separates it from its competitors is that it runs entirely on device and on premise — nothing leaves the building.

Swift On-device LLMs Whisper Xcode Privacy-first

For the patient

The patient-facing app delivers powerful statistical analysis of a person's own health data — computed right on their device, so the raw data never has to travel anywhere to become useful.

For the clinic

The clinic-facing app acts as a scribe, helping practitioners with the ever-increasing burden of paperwork. Consultation in, structured note out, without the evening spent catching up on documentation.

how it started

From a hackathon to a working build

01

The hackathon

January Capital Hackathon, hosted by UQIES. The team produced a markup using Lovable, an AI agent that builds websites from a single prompt.

02

The prototype

After the hackathon, a working prototype was developed in Swift — a real app rather than a mockup.

03

The models

Open-weight language models like Phi-4 and GPT-oss handle note generation. Whisper handles speech transcription, to keep transcripts medically accurate.
