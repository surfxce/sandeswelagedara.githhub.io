UQSLA · running for VP of Events

VP Events Manifesto

AGM · Wednesday 14 October · 5 min read

Suits n Sarees

  • I'm projecting over 2,000 people at Suits n Sarees in 2027, a 185% increase in three years.
  • This growth means we need a larger venue and a larger budget. I'm shortlisting the Plaza Ballroom at the Brisbane International Convention Centre, though I'm keeping other options open depending on cost. Past venues like Cloudland and The Warehouse can't fit 2,000 people, and I'll need to confirm whether venues like the Beaumont and Summa House can either.
  • Since people are averse to price increases, I'm looking at adjusting the distribution across ticket rounds (fewer Round 1 tickets, more in Round 2) rather than a flat price hike. Early bird pricing stays the same.
  • With a consistent 4-5% no-show rate, we may be able to sell some extra tickets depending on venue capacity limits.
  • A bigger turnout also means a bigger budget for events later in the year, similar to 2026, where SnS profit helped fund Fright Night, Spice Road and others.
  • I want to expand the bar beyond mimosas to include a wine and gin selection.
  • I'm aiming to recreate the 2024 floor-space ratio, which worked well, after 2025 felt too cramped.
  • Projected date: 23 July 2027, the Friday before semester starts.
  • Considering a custom ribbon-and-bead wristband as a nicer, keepsake-style alternative to a paper one, similar to how sit-down balls give out name tags to take home.

Spice Road

  • Keep, this event works.
  • Looking to invite the Malayalam Society for 2027.

Fright Night

  • Keep expanding. Interest has grown year on year, and it helps maintain strong relationships with the Nepalese Club and the Indian Students Club.

Sri Lankan New Year

  • Execution was strong this year, keep it largely unchanged.

Stars n Sails

  • Add a bar tab, or at minimum a drink ticket.

Sarong Party

  • What makes it unique is the choice between food or a drink rather than both.
  • Drinks were affordable ($7 a shot) and well received.
  • Considering a second Sarong Party in Semester 2, though I'll hold off unless it can stand on its own rather than just padding out the semester.

Market Day

  • We have a strong presence in Semester 1, but need one in Semester 2 too, especially with events like Fright Night and Spice Road happening then.

Trivia Night

  • I'd like SLA execs a little more involved. It's a small, low-stakes event, so this isn't a big ask.
  • Considering splitting the quiz deck with the Nepalese Club.

Lantern Making

  • Keep, a nice, low-stakes event.

Other Event Ideas

  • Movie night is a maybe. There isn't a strong lineup of Sri Lankan films to build one around.
  • Considering rebranding Ceylon Soiree into an end-of-semester party with our closest partner clubs, budget permitting.
  • QUT's Sri Lankan society is still quite small. May not be worth a formal relationship beyond a small mingling event.

Kothu and Karrom

  • Small logistics tweak: layering forks and napkins between food boxes to speed up service.

Charity Initiatives

  • Considering a Semester 1 fundraiser, potentially folded into Spice Road.
  • Looking at ongoing charitable causes across the year: women's rights, women in STEM, freedom of speech and press.
  • Serendib Night: "Serendib" is the old Persian name for Sri Lanka. Concept is a sit-down charity dinner, potentially co-organised with other clubs to split costs and organisation. Estimating 200 to 500 tickets. Minimal or no bar tab, with a dance floor, since it's not really an SLA event without dancing.
  • Exploring a date auction concept as part of this. Happy to discuss this directly with members and take feedback.

Marketing

  • Exploring a dedicated website (domain names run about $10/year) so members can see ticket round timing, upcoming events and the team, a more polished alternative to our current Linktree.
  • This would complement our social channels, not replace them. Reels and posts stay on Instagram, since that's still where our main traffic is.
  • A domain name also means more organised email: sponsorship enquiries to sponsorship@uqsla.com, secretary enquiries to secretary@uqsla.com, with automatic forwarding to keep inboxes manageable.
  • Considering a refreshed exec shirt design with the lion emblem. We'll fully subsidise one shirt per exec, with a partially subsidised second shirt as an option. Looking at breathable fabric (no polyester).
  • We won't be doing sponsored posts or ads.
  • Posts should go out on schedule, a post scheduler if that helps with consistency.
  • Keeping AI use in posts minimal. Members notice, and it doesn't land well.

Team Structure and Dynamic

  • Keeping the exec discount, it's good for morale.
  • I want execs to genuinely mingle and socialise, not just coordinate around tasks.
  • No idea is a dumb idea. I want open communication and to stay open to criticism.
  • Trusting execs to run, plan and organise events themselves keeps them engaged and invested, rather than sitting on the sidelines.
  • Using Notion (or similar) to clearly delineate responsibilities. As Events VP, I'll be doing a lot of delegating, and clarity here matters.
  • Playing to each exec's strengths and preferences.
  • I want everyone on the team involved beyond their specific title: Events VP helping with marketing content, Marketing VP helping with event setup and pack-up, and T3 staying just as visible and engaged as the rest of the team.
  • A smaller, genuinely committed team will always outperform a larger team with disengaged members. We'll run periodic check-ins on engagement, with clear expectations set up front. The goal is support and communication first, with turnover only as a last resort if it's genuinely not working out.
  • Regular exec bonding outside of meetings and events. I want to meet with every exec on the Events team individually to understand their goals, strengths and what they want out of the role.
  • Appointing welfare officers (or rotating this role) to look after exec wellbeing.
  • Giving execs real autonomy. If I can trust the judgement, I want to let people run with it, especially since I can't be everywhere at once.
  • Marketing and Events need a genuinely close relationship. We don't get events without marketing, and that relationship should be a priority.
  • Weekly updates: Marketing sharing what's coming (reels, posts) and Events sharing ticket round timing and upcoming events, so the whole team stays in the loop.
  • Looking at switching to Humanitix for ticketing, lower platform fees and a better backend for ticket releases.

Culture and Performance

  • Aiming for more regular performances and dance rehearsals across Spice Road, Fright Night and SnS.
  • More celebrations of Tamil culture where appropriate.

Budget Philosophy

  • Spending should stay mindful. I'm looking for ways to keep costs sensible so we can deliver more later in the year.
