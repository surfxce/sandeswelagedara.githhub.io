Team Planner

Who's on the team?

List last year's committee and this year's. Roles are the leadership (your T3, T5, T8…), most senior first. Teams are groups like Events or Marketing. Anyone on both exec lists is the same person; anyone on only one starts in Out for the other year.

One per line. To place someone straight away, add their role or team after a dash: Priya — Events. Otherwise you'll drag them in on the next screen.

There's always an Out box too, for anyone graduating, leaving, or not on the team that year. No need to list it. A team reports to the role that names it (Events → VP Events); change that under Teams.

Drag a bubble to move someone (on a phone, hold it first). Tap a bubble to add a photo or change their details. Click the name at the top to rename your society. Saved in this browser only.
